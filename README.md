# 🏰 Castle Bound

## Game Description

**Castle Bound** is an immersive, mystery-driven puzzle and riddle experience that invites players to navigate through the shadowed corridors of an ancient, ever-shifting castle. Each chamber unveils a new intellectual challenge — from cryptic mathematical conundrums to intricate logic-based enigmas — ensuring that no two encounters feel the same.

---

### 🎮 Core Experience

Players progress through a series of atmospheric **trails** (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, and beyond), each representing a deeper descent into the castle's secrets. As players advance, the castle itself transforms — introducing evolving visual themes, ambient soundscapes, and increasingly complex riddle categories that span mathematics, lateral thinking, wordplay, pattern recognition, and deductive logic.

---

### ✨ Key Features

- **Diverse Riddle Categories** — Every trail introduces a curated mix of puzzle types, including mathematical reasoning, logical deduction, spatial awareness, linguistic riddles, and abstract pattern challenges, each distinctly crafted to test different cognitive skills.

- **Progressive Difficulty Scaling** — Trails are structured with carefully calibrated difficulty curves, ranging from accessible introductory puzzles to deeply challenging brain-teasers designed for seasoned thinkers.

- **No Time Pressure** — Players are encouraged to think deeply and explore at their own pace. There are no countdown timers or urgency mechanics — only the quiet tension of the castle's atmosphere.

- **Intelligent Hint System** — A three-tier progressive hint mechanism gently nudges players toward the solution without spoiling the thrill of discovery. Each successive hint reveals a deeper layer of the puzzle's logic. Should all three hints be exhausted, the correct answer is gracefully revealed alongside a brief explanation, ensuring the player learns and moves forward without frustration.

- **Dynamic Scoring System** — Points are awarded based on puzzle-solving efficiency, hint conservation, and streak consistency. Players who solve riddles without assistance earn maximum points, rewarding both skill and perseverance.

- **Mysterious & Evolving Atmosphere** — Each trail features a unique visual theme — from fog-laden dungeons and candlelit libraries to moonlit towers and forgotten catacombs — accompanied by immersive sound effects including echoing footsteps, distant whispers, creaking doors, and ambient wind that deepen the sense of mystery.

- **Fluid Animations** — Smooth, atmospheric animations bring the castle to life, from flickering torchlight and drifting mist to satisfying puzzle-solving transitions and dramatic reveal sequences.

---

### 🎯 Who Is It For?

**Castle Bound** is designed for curious minds of all ages — casual players seeking a relaxing mental escape, puzzle enthusiasts craving variety, and anyone drawn to the allure of a good mystery. Whether you have five minutes or five hours, the castle awaits.
